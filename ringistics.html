Ringistics — Your Workday, Simplified

Ringistics is a simple work logging app designed for intermodal truck drivers.

Keep track of your shipments, trips, mileage, working hours, and pay without dealing with complicated spreadsheets or paperwork.

Ringistics remembers the places you visit, saves your trip information, and helps you keep your work organized.

Everything is stored directly on your device. The app works offline and does not require an internet connection or company system access.

Download Ringistics for Android

Getting Started

Step 1 — Install Ringistics

  1. Download the APK onto your Android phone or tablet.
  2. Open the downloaded file and follow the instructions to install it.
  3. If Android asks for permission to install apps from this source, allow the installation and continue.

Step 2 — Set Up Your Name

  1. Open Ringistics.
  2. Enter your name in the app settings.

That's all you need to get started.

You can enter your pay information and other settings later if you want to track your earnings.

How to Use Ringistics

Starting a Shipment

  1. Enter your shipment number.
  2. Start a leg for that shipment.
  3. Enter your trip information.

A shipment can contain more than one leg.

What Information Do You Need?

Only two fields are required when entering a leg:

  • Container number
  • Company miles

Everything else is optional.

Fill in additional information if you want to keep more detailed records.

Saved Places — Type It Once!

Ringistics remembers the places you enter.

  1. The first time you visit a location, enter its information and save it.
  2. The next time you visit that location, select it from Saved Places.

Ringistics automatically fills in the saved information for you.

No need to type the same location information over and over again.

Finishing a Shipment

  1. When you finish a leg, close that leg.
  2. If you have another leg on the same shipment, start the next leg.
  3. When the entire shipment is finished, close the shipment.

Important: You must close your current shipment before starting another shipment.

Android Installation Help

Android may show a warning because you are downloading the app from a website instead of the Google Play Store.

  1. Open the downloaded file. On Samsung, look in My Files → Downloads.
  2. If Android asks, tap Settings. Allow installation for the browser or file manager you used to open the download. The setting may say Allow from this source.
  3. Go back to the downloaded file and tap Install.

After installing, you can turn that permission off again in your phone's Settings. Search for Install unknown apps, select the same browser or file manager, and turn off Allow from this source.
