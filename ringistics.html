Ringistics.

Less typing. Easier trip paperwork.

Ringistics helps truck drivers keep track of shipments, legs, miles, work time, and pay. Keep your daily work together and print a clear trip sheet when you need it. The app works offline.

Download Ringistics for Android

Version 1.1.07 · APK download · About 12 MB

1. Install the app

  1. On your Android phone or tablet, tap Download Ringistics for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded APK file.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file app to install unknown apps. Then tap Install.
  4. Open Ringistics.

On Samsung, the file is usually in My Files → Downloads.

2. Set up with just your name

Enter your name. That is all you need for setup.

What do I have to fill in?

For the leg details, only container number and company miles are required. Company miles means the miles your company pays you for that leg. The other detail fields are optional.

3. Start a shipment, then a leg

A shipment is the job. A leg is one part of that job, such as taking a container from the rail to a customer.

  1. Enter the shipment number.
  2. Start a leg. Entering a shipment number alone does not start a leg.
  3. Fill in the container number and company miles. Add any other details you want to keep.
  4. Close the leg when it is finished. If the same shipment has more work, start another leg under that shipment.
  5. When all legs are finished, close the shipment before moving to the next shipment.

Remember: shipment number → start a leg → finish the legs → close the shipment.

Saved Places: type it once

Type a place in once and the app remembers it in Saved Places.

The next time you go there, select that saved place. Ringistics automatically fills in all the relevant information saved for it. You do not have to type the same place and details again.

For example: enter a customer’s location once. On your next visit, pick that customer from Saved Places.
